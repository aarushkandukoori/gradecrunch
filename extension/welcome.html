GradeCrunch

See all your grades and your QPA in one place. Before it reads anything, here is exactly what it does.

What it reads

Your own courses, assignments, scores and letter-grade cutoffs on canvas.cmu.edu, gradescope.com and autolab.andrew.cmu.edu. It uses the login you already have in this browser. It never sees or asks for your password, and it only reads these pages when the GradeCrunch site asks it to sync.

On SIO (s3.andrew.cmu.edu) it reads your Grades and QPA page: each semester's courses, units, grade option (letter or pass/fail), mid-semester and final grades, and your official QPA. That gives exact units and your past semesters.

It also reads each class's syllabus on Canvas (the Syllabus page, the home page and syllabus PDFs), because the real grading weights are often only written there. Canvas stores PDFs on inscloudgate.net, so reading them needs one more permission, which Chrome asks for when you click Continue.

Where it goes

Only to the GradeCrunch site open in your own browser, which saves it there. The extension never sends it to a server, and nothing is sold. The extension keeps a copy of your last successful sync in its own storage on this computer; removing the extension deletes it.

The site has an optional AI advisor, a chat about your grades. Only if you use it, your question and a summary of your grades go through the GradeCrunch server to OpenAI to get an answer. GradeCrunch keeps none of it.

GradeCrunch's use of this information follows the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Full details: privacy policy.

How to remove it

Right-click the GradeCrunch icon in the toolbar and choose Remove from Chrome, or open chrome://extensions and click Remove. That deletes everything the extension stored.
